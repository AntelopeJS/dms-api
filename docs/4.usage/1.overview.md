# Overview

The Overview (`/modules/api/overview`) answers one question first: is the API healthy, since when, and why not.

![The Overview with a Degraded verdict, two routes needing attention and the slowest routes](../images/overview.png)

## Health verdict

The hero card shows the verdict (**Healthy**, **Degraded** or **Down**), how long it has held and when it was checked, next to the four signals it is computed from over the last 15 minutes: the 5xx rate, the p95 latency, the traffic in requests per minute and the 4xx rate. The API is **Degraded** from 1% of server errors over 15 minutes and **Down** from 20% over 5 minutes.

Under the hero, a sentence names the routes above the thresholds, and **View server errors** opens the [Request logs](/docs/modules/api/usage/request-logs) narrowed to them.

## Blocks

| Block | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Needs attention | Routes that are failing (5xx), slow (p95 at or above the slow threshold) or noisy (many 4xx), with their main error. |
| Slowest routes | Routes ranked by average latency, with p95, max and call count. |
| Error share | The share of 4xx and 5xx over the period, compared with the period the toolbar picks. |
| Traffic & errors | Calls over time, with errors. |
| Responses by class | Calls per status class (2xx, 3xx, 4xx, 5xx). |

Every route and request is a link: a route opens in [Routes](/docs/modules/api/usage/routes), a request in its side panel, and an incident opens the Request logs narrowed to its own range (`?since=&until=`).

## Period and scope

The Overview, Request logs and Routes open on the same DMS page toolbar, at the same size: the scope chip on the left, then how long ago the page was updated, **Live / Pause**, the period, the comparison and the presets.

The period selector (1H, 24H, 7D, 30D) sets what the blocks cover. On the Overview and Routes, the comparison menu (**vs previous period ▾**) sets what the deltas and the "vs" figures compare with:

- **vs previous period**: the span of the same length right before (the default);
- **vs previous year**: the same span a year before;
- **No comparison**: no delta and no "vs" figure.

A comparison reaching beyond the retention compares with nothing rather than with zeros: a chart then shows no "vs" figure, and Routes says there is no data to compare with. The statistics are kept 90 days at most, so **vs previous year** says there is nothing a year back to compare with. The Request logs list requests and compare nothing.

The period is kept in the URL (`?api.period=last-24h`), and the comparison with it (`?api.compare=previous-year`); without them, a page opens on the last choice made on any of the three pages, else on the last 24 hours against the period before.

The scope chip (**Own routes · 15**) shows which routes the console watches. Change it on the [API settings](/docs/modules/api/usage/settings) page.

## Live mode

The Overview, Request logs and Routes share the DMS **Live / Pause** toggle (`L`):

- **Live** (the default): the page follows new requests as the DMS realtime channel announces them. Each block reads again at most every 5 s (Request logs), 10 s (Overview) or 15 s (Routes), every minute once a page shows a week or more, and on that clock while the realtime channel is down.
- **Paused**: nothing is read. The toggle says what was missed ("Paused · 40 new requests · 2 in 5xx"), the request tables keep their rows and offer "40 new requests · 2 in 5xx · Show". Resuming reads each block once.

The page also pauses by itself while its browser tab is hidden and while you type in the Tester. A request table holds its rows while one of them is open in the side panel and says why ("Table on hold · search active"). The choice applies to all three pages; `?live=0` opens a page paused.
